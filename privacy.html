Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Baldemar De Leon ("I," "me," or "we"), a licensed real estate agent with WrightHouse Group at Ebby Halliday Realtors, collects, uses, and protects the information you provide through this website (baldemardeleon.com) and related communications. Information We Collect

When you contact me through the website's contact form or otherwise reach out, I may collect:

  • Your name

  • Your phone number (optional)

I do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information, and I do not require it to help you.

How We Use Your Information

I use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and provide real estate services
  • Contact you by phone, email, or text message about your inquiry and your home buying, selling, leasing, or investing needs
  • Send you relevant updates, listings, or information you have requested
  • Improve how I serve clients

SMS / Text Messaging

If you provide your phone number and opt in through our contact form, you consent to receive text messages from Baldemar De Leon related to real estate services, including responses to your inquiry, appointment coordination, and relevant updates.

  • Message frequency varies based on your interaction with us.
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
  • You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message. After you reply STOP, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us.
  • For help, reply HELP or contact us using the information at the bottom of this policy.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information collected as part of SMS consent — including your phone number and opt-in — will not be sold, rented, or shared with any third parties for their own marketing. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent is never shared with third parties. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our business and deliver these communications (such as our CRM and messaging platform), solely to perform services on our behalf.

How We Share Information

I do not sell your personal information. I may share the information you provide only in these limited situations:

As stated above, phone numbers and SMS opt-in information are never shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

Your Choices
